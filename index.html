Help and support

What NOVA AI can do

Find services and records, explain what a record is for, show if it is digital, physical or both, and where the paper copy is kept.

What it cannot do

See personal accounts or balances, take payments, give legal advice, or waive fees. It will offer to pass your question to a council officer.

Need a person?

Use "Pass to a council officer" in the chat. For e-Services portal support: +260 211 428 600 (short code 2008) or info@eservices.gov.zm. Council contact details: To be confirmed with LCC.

Your privacy

You do not need to give a name or phone number to ask a question. Contact details are collected only if you choose to leave them for a reply.
